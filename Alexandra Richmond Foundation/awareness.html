Know The Signs

What everyone should know.

Early esophageal cancer often has no obvious symptoms, which is exactly why awareness matters. If something feels off, it's worth a conversation with your doctor. These are the signs, the risks, and the reason early detection changes everything.

Common Symptoms

  • Difficulty or pain when swallowing
  • Unexplained weight loss
  • Persistent heartburn or indigestion
  • Chest pressure or discomfort
  • Chronic cough or hoarseness

Risk Factors

  • Long-term acid reflux (GERD)
  • Barrett's esophagus
  • Smoking and heavy alcohol use
  • Obesity
  • Age over 50, more common in men

Why Early Detection Matters

Caught early, these cancers are far more treatable. Caught late, the odds drop sharply. Talking to a doctor about persistent symptoms can change everything. It is the single reason this Foundation exists.
